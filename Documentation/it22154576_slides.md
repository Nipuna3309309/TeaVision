### Slide 1: Problem Statement & Stakeholders
**Problem Statement & Stakeholders (Very Brief)**
* **Context:** Sri Lankan Tea Factory intake stations and estate quality control.
* **Stakeholders:** 
  * *Primary users:* Tea leaf inspectors, estate managers.
  * *Secondary users:* Factory workers, agricultural extension officers.
  * *Decision makers:* Factory owners.
* **Observed issue:** Manual visual inspection of fresh tea leaves is slow, highly subjective, and prone to human error. Furthermore, crucial agricultural guidelines are trapped in heavy manuals and hard to access on the field.
* **Why it matters:** Accepting poor-quality raw material (e.g., coarse plucks, damaged leaves) directly degrades the final manufactured tea grade, leading to severe revenue losses and brand damage.

### Slide 2: Research Gap & Objectives
**Research Gap & Objectives (Recap)**
* **Gap:** Existing automated agricultural solutions often use "black-box" AI that lacks explainability for farmers, lose detection accuracy on microscopic elements (like tiny fresh buds) when scaling down images, and don't integrate real-time expert agricultural knowledge alongside the analysis.
* **Main objective:** To engineer an explainable, highly accurate visual inspection and knowledge retrieval system utilizing SAHI (Slicing Aided Hyper Inference) for small object detection, OpenCV for interpretable feature extraction, and RAG for instant semantic search capabilities.

---

### Slide 3: Solution Overview
**Solution Overview (What You Built)**
* **Solution summary:** A tripartite AI system for the tea industry featuring automated high-resolution leaf detection, mathematically explainable quality grading, and an interactive semantic agricultural search engine.
* **Key features delivered:** 
  1. Tea Leaf Detection System using YOLOv8 + SAHI.
  2. "Grade My Tea" Classification mathematically driven by 25 OpenCV hand-crafted features.
  3. "Ceylon Tea Knowledge Base" RAG subsystem for semantic SOP queries.
* **Non-functional focus:** Usability (seamless mobile-to-desktop capture sync), Reliability (SAHI prevents resizing data loss), and Explainability (open model comparisons and feature extraction breakdowns).
* **Current completion status:** ~95%+ (Models trained, integrated, and endpoints live).
